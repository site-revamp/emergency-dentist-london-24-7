When to seek urgent care

Is your dental problem an emergency?

Sudden pain, swelling or damage to a tooth can be distressing. If you are unsure what to do, call us and explain what has happened.

Speak to the team
01

Severe toothache

Persistent or intense tooth pain that needs prompt dental assessment.

02

Swelling or a dental abscess

Gum or facial swelling, tenderness or signs of a dental infection.

03

Broken, chipped or damaged teeth

A tooth damaged by an accident, a fall or biting something hard.

04

Knocked-out or displaced tooth

Dental trauma that needs urgent advice and assessment.

05

Lost fillings, crowns or restorations

A lost or damaged restoration causing discomfort or leaving a tooth exposed.

06

Wisdom tooth pain and dental injuries

Discomfort around wisdom teeth or injuries affecting the teeth and mouth.
