Emergency Dental Experts Golders Green Homepage Redesign
Website Overview
Emergency Dental Experts Golders Green is a private emergency dental clinic located at 50 The Vale, Golders Green, London. The practice provides 24/7 emergency dental treatment, walk-in appointments and urgent dental care without requiring prior patient registration.

The redesigned homepage focuses on immediate patient needs, making emergency treatment options, pricing, clinical credentials and contact methods easier to understand. The overall direction prioritises fast access to emergency dental assistance, patient reassurance and telephone enquiries.
Main Improvements
1. Clearer Emergency Appointment Pathways
- Prioritised emergency dental appointments and 24/7 availability.
- Made telephone and WhatsApp contact options more prominent.
- Highlighted walk-in access without requiring prior registration.
Problem addressed: Patients experiencing dental emergencies need immediate instructions. The previous website required visitors to navigate extensive information to understand how to access urgent treatment.
2. Improved Treatment and Pricing Presentation
- Organised emergency dental services into clearer treatment categories.
- Made consultation pricing, starting from £35 during weekday hours and £45 out of hours, easier to identify.
- Improved the presentation of common emergencies, including toothache, abscesses, broken teeth and dental trauma.
Problem addressed: Treatment information and pricing were not presented as a sufficiently clear, easy-to-scan decision-making experience.
3. Stronger Trust and Clinical Credibility
- Improved the visibility of professional credentials and regulatory information.
- Highlighted the practice's 24/7 emergency care proposition.
- Strengthened the presentation of patient reassurance, professional experience and supporting trust information.
Problem addressed: Important trust signals were not sufficiently integrated into the emergency appointment journey, potentially making it harder for new patients to feel confident about seeking treatment.
Design Improvements
- Visual Hierarchy: Created a clearer content structure that prioritises emergency assistance, treatment availability and contact actions.
- Colour and Typography: Applied a consistent visual system to improve readability and reinforce the professional healthcare identity.
- Homepage Layout: Organised treatment information, pricing, trust elements and supporting content into more clearly defined sections.
- User Experience: Reduced friction in accessing telephone enquiries, WhatsApp contact and walk-in appointment information.
- Mobile Experience: Prioritised accessible contact actions and readable treatment information for patients seeking urgent assistance on mobile devices.
- Content Consistency: Improved the presentation of emergency services, additional treatments, pricing and clinic information throughout the homepage.
